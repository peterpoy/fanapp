imafan initial idea
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Philadelphia flyers have once again become my obsession. I want to follow all their teams and all their prospects, and I want to know when they're playing close to me so that I can try to watch them play. There's a lot to unpact with this type of fandom, but I can start wtih some basic functionalities which I'll describe below:

1. Calendar including all of the Philadelphia Flyers' games, along with Lehigh Valley Phantoms games, Reading Royals Games.
2. Explanation as to why those three teams are relevant to the Philadelphia Flyers
3. Prospect tracker including all of the Flyers' drafted players
4. Prospect calendars
5. Prospect stats
6. Results of the games synced to the calendar
7. League standings of the three flyers' organizations and the prospects' teams
8. Tickets to the games listed in the calendars
9. Distances to the arenas that they play at
10. make it generalized so it's easy to do this for any professional sports organization
